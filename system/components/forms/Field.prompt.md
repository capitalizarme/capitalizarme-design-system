One-line: the underline form field used in every Capitalizarme form.

```jsx
<Field id="name" label="¿Cómo te llamas?" placeholder="Camila" />
<Field id="email" label="¿Cuál es tu email?" type="email" />
<Button type="submit">Siguiente</Button>
```

- Labels are questions in *tú* form, exactly as the real booking form writes them.
- Focus moves the bottom border to brand blue; the global focus ring still applies.
- Disabled dims the input only — the label keeps full contrast.
