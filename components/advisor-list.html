Advisor list component

The "Mensajes" rail — a real advisor-thread list, each row an initials avatar (brand-blue circle, white bold initials) + name + truncated last message, with a tinted background on the selected row. Copied from ui_kits/app-react/components/AdvisorList.jsx: the direct product continuation of ContactForm.astro's whole purpose (booking time with an advisor) carried on as an ongoing conversation — see ui_kits/app/README.md "Messaging surface". Names/roles/messages are the system's real evidenced advisor copy, not filler (DESIGN.md §9). Depends on colors_and_type.css tokens plus the rules below. Note the class here is .meta-col (the source JSX/CSS uses the generic .meta, renamed to avoid colliding with this preview page's own .meta label style).
