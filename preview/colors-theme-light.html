Light theme — surfaces & text roles

Capitalizarme's source has no dark theme — every surface evidenced is white or a pale blue tint. This card documents the semantic roles derived from that: background, card surface, tint panel, and the three text colors in play (pure black body copy, navy emphasis, muted secondary).
